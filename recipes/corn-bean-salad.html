Fiesta Corn & Black Bean Salad

Prep Time: 15 mins | Cook Time: 5 mins | Yield: 8 servings

Ingredients

  • 4 cups sweet corn (charred fresh or canned, drained)
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, rinsed and drained
  • 1 large red bell pepper, finely diced
  • 1/2 medium red onion, finely diced
  • 1/2 cup fresh cilantro, finely chopped
  • 1 fresh jalapeño, seeded and finely diced
  • 2 fresh avocados, diced
  • 1/3 cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • Juice of 2 fresh limes (about 3-4 tbsp)
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp honey or maple syrup
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/2 tsp chili powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1 bag sturdy tortilla chips (for serving)

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a heavy skillet over high heat. Add corn in a single layer and let sit for 2-3 minutes to get a smoky char, then stir and transfer to a large mixing bowl.
  2. Add rinsed black beans, diced red bell pepper, red onion, jalapeño, and chopped cilantro to the bowl with the corn.
  3. In a small bowl or jar, whisk together the olive oil, lime juice, minced garlic, honey, cumin, chili powder, salt, and black pepper until emulsified.
  4. Pour the dressing over the salad and toss thoroughly to coat. Cover and chill in the refrigerator for at least 2 hours before serving.
  5. Gently fold in the diced avocados right before serving alongside sturdy tortilla chips.
