Grandma's Lassies

A cherished family heirloom recipe for rich chocolate, nut, and cream cheese lassie bars.

⏱️ Prep: 30 mins + Chill 🔥 Bake: 20 mins 🔥 Temp: 350°F

Ingredients

Crust

Filling

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, mix together the cream cheese, 2 ½ cups butter, and 2 ½ cups flour until smooth.
  2. Chill the crust mixture in the refrigerator.
  3. Once chilled, shape the dough into ½ inch balls and press them into baking tins, then re-chill.
  4. For the filling: In a saucepan over low heat, combine the chocolate chips, 1 ⅓ cups sugar, 4 tbsp butter, 4 tbsp milk, and 4 beaten eggs. Cook over low heat until completely melted and combined.
  5. Remove filling from heat, then add the vanilla extract and chopped nuts.
  6. Divide the filling into the chilled crust tins.
  7. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 20 minutes.
