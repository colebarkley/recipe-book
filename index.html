Chocolate Chip Banana Muffins

Moist, bakery-style muffins perfect for using up ripe bananas.

⏱️ Prep: 10 mins | 🔥 Bake: 18–22 mins | 🧁 Yield: 12 muffins

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe bananas, mashed
  • ⅓ cup melted butter
  • ¾ cup granulated sugar
  • 1 large egg, beaten
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp baking soda & pinch of salt
  • 1 ½ cups all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a 12-cup muffin tin.
  2. In a bowl, mash bananas with a fork and stir in melted butter.
  3. Mix in sugar, egg, and vanilla extract.
  4. Sprinkle baking soda and salt over mixture and stir in.
  5. Gently fold in flour until combined, then fold in chocolate chips.
  6. Divide batter into muffin cups and bake for 18–22 minutes.
