Low-Sugar Cookie Dough Protein Bars

Prep Time: 15 mins
Freeze Time: 1 hour
Yield: 10 bars
Category: Snacks

Ingredients

Cookie Dough Base:

  • 1 1/2 cups almond flour
  • 2 to 3 scoops whey protein isolate powder
  • 1/3 cup maple syrup
  • 1/4 cup coconut oil, melted
  • 1/2 cup peanut butter
  • 1/4 cup dark chocolate chips
  • 1/2 tsp salt

Chocolate Coating:

  • 1/2 cup dark chocolate chips
  • 1 tsp coconut oil

Instructions

  1. Mix Base: In a large bowl, mix almond flour, protein powder, peanut butter, maple syrup, melted coconut oil, and salt until a thick dough forms.
  2. Fold: Fold in 1/4 cup dark chocolate chips.
  3. Shape & Freeze: Press dough tightly into an 8x8-inch pan lined with parchment paper. Freeze for 1 hour until solid.
  4. Melt Topping: Combine 1/2 cup dark chocolate chips and 1 tsp coconut oil in a microwave-safe bowl. Heat in 30-second increments, stirring until smooth. Spread over chilled base.
  5. Set & Cut: Return to the fridge for 10 minutes until the top shell hardens. Slice into bars and store sealed in the fridge or freezer.
