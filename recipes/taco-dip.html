Taco Dip

Nina's classic party dip recipe

Prep: 15 mins • Category: Appetizer • Yield: 8-10 servings

Ingredients

  • 2 (8 oz) packages cream cheese, softened
  • 1 (16 oz) container sour cream
  • 1 (1.25 oz) packet taco seasoning mix
  • 1 jar taco sauce
  • Shredded lettuce
  • Chopped tomatoes
  • Black olives, sliced
  • Cheddar cheese, grated
  • Taco chips (for serving)

Instructions

  1. Mix first 3 ingredients (softened cream cheese, sour cream, taco seasoning) together until smooth.
  2. Spread on a large pie plate or serving platter.
  3. Top with shredded lettuce, chopped tomatoes, sliced black olives, and grated cheddar cheese.
  4. Drizzle with taco sauce. Serve with taco chips.
