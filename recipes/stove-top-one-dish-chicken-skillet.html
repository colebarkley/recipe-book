Stove Top® One-Dish Chicken Skillet

Quick & easy weekday skillet dinner

Prep: 5 mins • Total: 25 mins • Yield: 6 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ cups hot water
  • ¼ cup (½ stick) butter or margarine, melted
  • 1 pkg (6 oz) STOVE TOP Stuffing Mix for Chicken
  • 1 ½ lbs boneless skinless chicken tenderloins
  • 1 can (10 ¾ oz) condensed cream of mushroom soup
  • ⅓ cup sour cream
  • 1 cup sliced mushrooms

Instructions

  1. Mix hot water, butter, and stuffing mix in a bowl; set aside.
  2. Spray a nonstick skillet with cooking spray. Add chicken tenderloins and sliced mushrooms; cook on medium heat 5 minutes on each side.
  3. Mix condensed soup and sour cream together; pour over chicken. Top with the prepared stuffing.
  4. Reduce heat to low; cover and cook 10 minutes, or until chicken is cooked through.
Family Note: Added sliced mushrooms during the skillet searing step.
