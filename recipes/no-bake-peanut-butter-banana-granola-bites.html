No-Bake PB & Banana Granola Bites

Easy, kid-friendly snack bites perfect for school lunches and quick energy.

⏱️ Prep: 15 mins ❄️ Chill: 20 mins 🍪 Yield: 12–15 bites

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. In a medium mixing bowl, combine rolled oats, peanut butter, honey, mashed banana, and vanilla extract until well combined.
  2. Gently fold in mini chocolate chips or any extra mix-ins.
  3. Chill the bowl in the refrigerator for 15 to 20 minutes to firm up the mixture.
  4. Roll the chilled mixture into 1-inch balls.
  5. Store in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 7 days, or freeze for up to 3 months.
